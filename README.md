# superstore-powerbi-dashboard
Power BI sales dashboard analysing Superstore sales, profit and trends (2014-2017)

## Business questions
- How much did the store sell, and how much profit did it keep?
- Which regions and product categories sell the most?
- Which individual products are the best sellers?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which products or discounts are losing the store money?

## About the data
The dataset is the Superstore retail sales data, covering orders from 2014 to 2017. Each row is one item sold, with its sales, profit, discount, region, category and dates.

## What I did
1. Cleaned the data. I found about 1,708 rows where the order date and ship date were swapped, and fixed them.
2.Built a date table so sales can be analyzed by year and quarter.
3.Wrote DAX measures for Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders and Average Order Value.
4. Designed the dashboard with summary cards, charts for region, category and top products, a quarterly sales trend, and a Region filter.

## Key findings
- The store made about  $2.30M in sales  and $286K in profit, which is a profit margin of around 12%.
-Technology is the top category by sales.
- West and East are the strongest regions, and South is the weakest.
- The Tables sub-category sells well but runs at a loss.
- Higher discounts reduce profit

## Tools used
Power BI, DAX, Data modelling
